Project 01

Printing Book — A Short Guide to Five Printing Methods

Print design project
Editorial — Print Design

Overview

A 9-page print booklet explaining five printing methods — offset lithography, flexography, digital printing, large format, and screen printing. Designed with a clean editorial layout, custom diagrams, comparison tables, and a glossary, making technical print knowledge accessible and easy to compare.
